Institutional Framework & Methodology

Combating backtest overfitting through statistical rigor and open-source validation.

The Core Problem

In quantitative finance, backtests are frequently overfitted to historical noise. A strategy may yield extraordinary in-sample Sharpe ratios while failing catastrophically under out-of-sample distribution shifts, transaction friction, and regime changes.

Validation Standards

Every paper published or featured under Arben Labs is evaluated against strict econometric standards:

  • Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO): Assessing the likelihood that an optimal in-sample strategy performs below median out-of-sample.
  • Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR): Adjusting performance metrics for non-normality and the number of trials attempted.
  • Code Transparency: Requiring reproducible Python/R execution pipelines for every submission.
