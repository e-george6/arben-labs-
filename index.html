Mission & Scope

Most backtested quantitative strategies achieve extraordinary historical performance but suffer catastrophic failure under real-world distribution shifts, market friction, and regime changes. Arben Labs was founded to bridge the gap between empirical backtesting and statistical reality.

We provide a open platform for student researchers, independent quantitative developers, and undergraduate academics to publish reproducible, high-signal research in computational finance and econometric modeling.

Focus Domains

  • Quantitative Finance: Algorithmic execution, risk management, and market microstructure.
  • Machine Learning: Time-series forecasting, non-stationary market modeling, and neural architectures.
  • Econometric Validation: Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO) and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) protocols.
  • Neuroeconomics: Interdisciplinary market behavior and cognitive decision models under uncertainty.

Peer Review & Editorial Standards

  • Code Reproducibility: All submissions must include an open-source GitHub repository with clean Python/R code.
  • Overfit Verification: Strategies are evaluated against data leaks, curve-fitting, and out-of-sample stress testing.
  • Open Access: Authors retain full copyright under Creative Commons (CC BY 4.0) licensing.
  • LaTeX Formatting: Papers must follow standardized academic typesetting guidelines.
